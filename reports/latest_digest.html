Top 5 da leggere subito (ordinati per impatto)

1) Successful Pregnancy After Planned Pregnancy-Oriented TKI Interruption and Embryo Transfer Following Ponatinib Re-Consolidation in Chronic-Phase Chronic Myeloid Leukemia.

🔬 Trial clinico📰 EJHaem
  • Impact score: 18
  • Focus clinico: TFR / stop terapia, Dose strategy / consolidamento
  • PMID: 42840123 | 10.1002/jha2.70426
  • Link PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42840123/
  • Tipo di studio/popolazione: Pregnancy after unsuccessful treatment-free remission (TFR) in chronic-phase chronic myeloid leukemia (CP-CML) remains challenging.
  • Risultato chiave: Ponatinib re-consolidation enabled a second TKI-free interval, with MMR maintained but deep molecular response unstable.
  • Rilevanza clinica: Further ponatinib re-consolidation restored MR4.5; ponatinib was stopped before embryo transfer, resulting in pregnancy and live birth without TKI exposure.

2) The evolving landscape of tyrosine kinase inhibitors in chronic-phase chronic myeloid leukemia: A 25-year journey.

📄 Altro📰 Blood reviews
  • Impact score: 11
  • Focus clinico: Resistenza / T315I / mutazioni, TFR / stop terapia, Sequencing / linee di terapia
  • PMID: 42875330 | 10.1016/j.blre.2026.101436
  • Link PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42875330/
  • Tipo di studio/popolazione: BCR::ABL1 tyrosine kinase inhibitors (TKIs) have transformed the treatment landscape of chronic-phase chronic myeloid leukemia, normalizing life expectancy for most patients.
  • Risultato chiave: For frontline therapy, generically available first-generation BCR::ABL1 TKI imatinib is a rational and cost-effective approach to optimize survival.
  • Rilevanza clinica: Considerable finesse is warranted to tailor a range of efficacious and well-tolerated therapies to the individual needs of patients.

3) Perioperative care of patients with chronic myeloid leukemia on tyrosine kinase inhibitors.

📖 Review📰 Frontiers in oncology
  • Impact score: 10
  • Focus clinico: Safety e comorbidità, Sintesi (review/linee guida)
  • PMID: 42857533 | 10.3389/fonc.2026.1899207
  • Link PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42857533/
  • Tipo di studio/popolazione: Patients with chronic myeloid leukemia (CML) are increasingly undergoing surgical procedures as long-term survival improves with tyrosine kinase inhibitor (TKI) therapy.
  • Risultato chiave: This narrative review aims to synthesize current evidence regarding perioperative risk assessment and management in patients with CML receiving TKIs.
  • Rilevanza clinica: Perioperative management in CML requires individualized multidisciplinary planning integrating hematologic, anesthetic, surgical, and pharmacologic considerations to optimize surgical safety while maintaining disease control.

4) Rethinking cardiovascular risk stratification in TKI-treated chronic myeloid leukemia.

📄 Altro📰 Cancer treatment and research communications
  • Impact score: 9
  • Focus clinico: Safety e comorbidità
  • PMID: 42833183 | 10.1016/j.ctarc.2026.101473
  • Link PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42833183/
  • Tipo di studio/popolazione: Current cardiovascular management relies on general population risk scores and ankle-brachial index (ABI) measurement, tools not designed to capture TKI-specific vascular biology or detect subclinical arterial damage.
  • Risultato chiave: We appraise cardiovascular risk stratification tools and vascular imaging modalities in the context of TKI-treated CML, and propose an integrated algorithm combining dual risk scoring, ABI, and targeted femoral duplex ultrasound (DUS).
  • Rilevanza clinica: Vascular outcome reporting in TKI trials is hampered by terminological inconsistency: Major Adverse Limb Events (MALE), the standard limb-specific endpoint in contemporary cardiovascular trials, has never been adopted in any TKI/CML publication.

5) Tyrosine kinase inhibitor use and risk of neurodegenerative disease: A Swedish population-based cohort study.

🏥 Real-world📰 Alzheimer's & dementia : the journal of the Alzheimer's Association
  • Impact score: 6
  • Focus clinico: Altro
  • PMID: 42860391 | 10.1002/alz.71916
  • Link PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42860391/
  • Tipo di studio/popolazione: CML patients ≥50 years at diagnosis initiating TKI therapy were identified, alongside five comparators without CML matched on sex, age, and residence at diagnosis.
  • Risultato chiave: A total of 1322 CML cases were included in the study.
  • Rilevanza clinica: 1188 initiated imatinib (of which 41 experienced an NDD event), 541 dasatinib (15 events), 390 nilotinib (five events), 174 bosutinib, 71 ponatinib, and 41 asciminib.
