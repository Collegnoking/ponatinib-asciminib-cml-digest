## Top 5 da leggere subito (ordinati per impatto)

### 1) From Solid Organ Malignancies to Dual Leukaemias: Sequential CLL and CML in a Cancer Survivor.
- **Rivista**: European journal of case reports in internal medicine | **Tipo**: Altro
- **Impact score**: 4
- **Focus clinico**: TFR / stop terapia, Safety e comorbidità, Sequencing / linee di terapia
- PMID: 42266392 | 10.12890/2026_006813
- Link PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42266392/

- Tipo di studio/popolazione: He began treatment with Ibrutinib in 2018 due to bulky symptomatic adenopathy and later switched to acalabrutinib due to arthralgias.
- Risultato chiave: Following a hospitalization in 2022 for coronavirus disease 2019, he was found to have chronic myeloid leukaemia (CML), confirmed by BCR-ABL positivity and bone marrow biopsy.
- Rilevanza clinica: Imatinib was started for CML but due to potential drug-induced pulmonary toxicity, switched to asciminib.

### 2) Selective HDAC6 inhibition perturbs autophagy and enhances integrated stress response-mediated immunogenic apoptosis in chronic myeloid leukemia.
- **Rivista**: Biomedicine & pharmacotherapy = Biomedecine & pharmacotherapie | **Tipo**: Preclinico / laboratorio
- **Impact score**: 2
- **Focus clinico**: Resistenza / T315I / mutazioni
- PMID: 42284888 | 10.1016/j.biopha.2026.119652
- Link PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42284888/

- Tipo di studio/popolazione: Chronic myeloid leukemia (CML) is driven by the BCR-ABL1 fusion oncoprotein and is managed with tyrosine kinase inhibitors (TKIs).
- Risultato chiave: Autophagy-mediated survival signaling contributes to therapeutic resistance in CML.
- Rilevanza clinica: These findings identify HDAC6-driven autophagy as a therapeutically exploitable vulnerability in CML that, when combined with asciminib, triggers ISR-dependent immunogenic apoptosis.
